## Testing Thought Leaders

This investigative testing approach builds upon the foundational work of several key contributors to the testing community:

### **Michael Bolton**

- **Scenario Testing Framework** - The THEME → SETUP → ACTIVITIES → ORACLES → VARIATIONS structure
- **"Breaking the Test Case Addiction"** series - Moving beyond scripted test cases to rich exploration
- **Realistic user investigation** principles that guide our scenario-based content
- **Context-Driven Testing** school co-founder
- **Rapid Software Testing** methodology co-founder (with James Bach)

**Key Contributions to Our Approach:**

- Rich scenario content with realistic user contexts
- Guided flexibility that encourages discovery
- Investigation rather than verification mindset
- Breaking away from rigid test case execution

### **James Bach**

- **Heuristic Test Strategy Model (HTSM)** - Systematic thinking frameworks for test discovery
- **Exploratory Testing** methodologies and principles
- **Context-Driven Testing** philosophy that underlies our adaptive approach
- **Rapid Software Testing** methodology co-founder (with Michael Bolton)

**Key Contributions to Our Approach:**

- Systematic heuristic thinking models
- Context-driven testing philosophy
- Exploratory testing as skilled investigation
- Rapid feedback and learning cycles

### **Jon Bach**

- **Session-Based Test Management (SBTM)** - Time-boxed testing sessions with clear charters and accountability
- **Structured exploratory testing** approaches that provide our session framework

**Key Contributions to Our Approach:**

- Time-boxed session structure
- Charter-based exploration
- Accountability and documentation frameworks
- Structured approach to exploratory testing

### **Elisabeth Hendrickson**

- **Agile Testing** principles that inform our integration with development workflows
- **Risk-based testing** approaches that guide our prioritisation strategies

**Key Contributions to Our Approach:**

- Whole-team quality approach
- Risk-based prioritisation
- Agile integration patterns
- Collaborative testing practices

### **Cem Kaner**

- **Context-Driven Testing** school foundational principles
- **Testing as investigation** rather than verification mindset

**Key Contributions to Our Approach:**

- Context-driven testing principles
- Investigation over verification
- Questioning assumptions and adapting approaches
- Focus on information discovery

## Our Synthesis

Our **Investigative Testing** approach respectfully combines these proven methodologies into a unified framework suited to marine licensing domain testing while preserving the essential insights and principles from each contributor.

### **Integrated Methodology**

This approach integrates the best aspects of multiple testing methodologies:

- **Session-Based Testing Management (SBTM)** - Time-boxed structure with clear accountability
- **Scenario Testing (Bolton's approach)** - Rich, realistic user contexts with guided flexibility
- **Heuristic-Driven Exploration** - Systematic discovery using HTSM and domain models
- **User Journey Investigation** - Complete workflow testing from authentic user perspectives

### **Marine Licensing Adaptation**

While preserving the essential insights from these thought leaders, we've adapted their approaches specifically for:

- **Marine licensing domain complexity** - Regulatory requirements and specialist user needs
- **Government service context** - Accessibility, inclusion, and public accountability requirements
- **Mixed team composition** - Domain experts leading investigation with quality engineering support
- **Automation-first foundation** - Investigation that complements rather than replaces systematic validation

## Using These Foundations Responsibly

### **Respectful Application**

When applying these methodologies:

- **Credit the source** - Always acknowledge the thought leaders whose work we build upon
- **Preserve essential insights** - Don't dilute the core principles in adaptation
- **Share learnings** - Contribute back to the testing community when possible
- **Continuous learning** - Keep developing understanding of these foundational approaches

### **Adaptation Guidelines**

- **Context matters** - Adapt methodologies to your specific domain and constraints
- **Preserve intent** - Maintain the underlying goals while changing implementation
- **Document changes** - Be clear about how you've modified approaches and why
- **Measure effectiveness** - Validate that adaptations deliver intended value
